Expensee

Android · iOS

Simple expense and income tracking. No account, no sign-up – your data stays on your phone.

iOS – coming soon

Features

  • Quick entry of expenses and income with categories, custom categories with icon and color
  • Recurring payments (rent, subscriptions, salary) with reminders
  • Balance, statistics and overview by month, year or all time
  • Export to CSV and Excel, import from a spreadsheet template and backup
  • App lock with PIN, fingerprint or Face ID, and hidden amounts
  • 14 languages and several themes including dark

Free, Pro with one payment

Expensee is free to download. Pro unlocks with a single payment – no subscription, no ads. It adds the Year and All periods, unlimited recurring payments and categories, full-history export and additional themes.

Privacy

Expensee has no server and no user accounts and does not connect to the internet. Everything you record stays on your phone. Details in the Privacy Policy.
